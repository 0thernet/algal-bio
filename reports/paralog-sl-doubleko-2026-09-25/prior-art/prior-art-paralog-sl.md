# Prior art — paralog synthetic lethality, context->dependency -> combinatorial holdout

Verdict: **PARTIALLY_KNOWN method + NOVEL rigor axis.** The association method
itself is published; what this campaign adds is registered provenance,
dual-library conjunctive discovery, and evaluation against datasets that
postdate every published predictor. Sources: independent web prior-art lane
(read-only; no holdout data fetched) plus the papers below.

## Direct predecessors

- **De Kegel & Ryan 2021**, Cell Systems 12:1144-1159.e6 (DOI
  10.1016/j.cels.2021.08.006). The critical prior art: their SL training labels
  were generated by exactly this campaign's discovery step - DepMap 20Q2 CERES
  screens x molecular contexts (paralog loss -> partner dependency), loss
  defined by expression log2(TPM+1)<1 or z<-4, deep CN deletion, or damaging
  mutation; ~36.6k pairs at >=20% identity; ~3,634 annotatable (>=10 loss
  events), ~127 labeled SL; 22 pair-level features; RF classifier validated
  against a consensus of four combinatorial screens (47/50 consensus pairs in
  their top 5%); wet-validated ASF1A/ASF1B and COPS7A/COPS7B. They also
  quantified the selection bias of combinatorial screens and showed most
  combinatorial hits are cell-line specific. Our DEL/EXPR_LOW/LOF triad maps
  onto their loss triad, including the identical expression threshold.
- **De Kegel & Ryan 2019**, PLoS Genet 15:e1008466 - origin of the paradigm.
- **Kebabci et al. 2026** (Ryan lab), bioRxiv 10.64898/2026.01.19.700065 -
  most threatening prior art: cell-line-specific paralog SL prediction with
  per-line features; evaluated on Parrish/Ito/Klingbeil; 1,005-line prediction
  matrix on figshare 10.6084/m9.figshare.31058182. Active competitor.
- **Köferle et al. 2022**, Cell Reports 39:110636 - genome-wide
  biomarker x dependency correlation in Avana; >2,000 paralog candidates;
  validated CSTF2/2T, FAM50A/B, DNAJC15/19, RPP25/RPP25L.
- **Lord, Quinn & Ryan 2020**, eLife 9:e58925 - driver->dependency
  validation across 4 datasets incl. cross-modality, disjoint panels;
  ~14% validated. Methodological precedent for cross-dataset evaluation.
- ISLE (Lee 2018), DAISY (Jerby-Arnon 2014), DiscoverSL (Das 2019),
  SINaTRA, SLant - SL predictors benchmarked in De Kegel Table S7.
- Collateral-lethality ancestors: Nijhawan 2012 (CYCLOPS), Muller 2012/2015,
  Kryukov/Mavrakis 2016 (MTAP->PRMT5/WDR77).

## What is NOT new

- Context->dependency mining for paralog SLs in DepMap (De Kegel 2021,
  Köferle 2022, Kebabci 2026).
- The conclusion that such associations predict combinatorial-SL calls
  (De Kegel validated exactly this).
- ML prediction of cell-line-specific SL (Kebabci 2026).

## What IS new

1. Registered, timestamped, frozen candidate list - nobody in this
   literature has done prospective registration.
2. Avana AND KY dual-library concordance as a *discovery* requirement.
3. Context-conditional holdout predictions: partner single-KO lethal in
   context-positive lines vs pair-level SL in context-negative lines -
   an asymmetric mapping unconditional predictors do not make.
4. Evaluation against datasets postdating all published predictors
   (in4mer 2024 harmonised re-scoring; Flister Nov 2025, 36,648 pairs).
5. Uniform re-scoring under the in4mer unified table rather than
   comparing published hit lists.

## Holdout corpus

| dataset | pairs | lines | scores |
|---|---|---|---|
| in4mer unified (figshare 24243832 Supp Table 1) | all 5 legacy studies + in4mer | PC9, HeLa, A549, HT29, OVCAR8, A375, MeWo, RPE1, HCT116, U2OS, K562, MELJUSO, + Ito 11 lines | dLFC + Cohen's d |
| Flister 2025 Table S2 | 36,648 | NCI-H1299, MDA-MB-231 | dKO vs additive expectation |
| pgPEN Table S4 (PMC8534300 supplements) | 1,030 | PC9, HeLa | GI < -0.5, FDR < 0.1; sKO arms |

Pitfalls registered: asymmetric vs symmetric pairs (DKO scores are unordered);
pair-universe mismatch per dataset; multi-targeting guides (Fortin 2019) -
excluded in prep; damaging-mutation != LoF - replaced by LikelyLoF contexts;
EXPR_LOW<1 can mark lineage-not-expressed rather than lost - mitigated by
lineage-spread qualification; base rates ~3.5-12%; holdout lines are mostly
shared with discovery (assay-level, not cohort-level independence).

Referee-safe claim: "registered paralog-loss-context->dependency associations
discovered from concordant single-KO evidence in two independent CRISPR
libraries, evaluated against harmonised combinatorial screens including
datasets generated after registration - quantifying the transferability and
boundary conditions of DepMap-derived paralog synthetic-lethal candidates."
Forbidden phrasings: "first prediction of paralog SLs"; "novel associations
discovered in unseen data" (the holdout is sealed to us, not to the field).
