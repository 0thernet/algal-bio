# Independent LMNA dataset eligibility audit

Metadata-only audit for the fixed discovery panel. Completed 2026-09-24. No new expression values or gene-specific outcomes were inspected. GEO metadata and processed-file headers were read; for GSE126458, only identifier/annotation columns were extracted under an explicit parent instruction. There was no paid inference, compute, credential use, or external contact.

GSE126458 exact maps are in `GSE126458.frozen-input-map.json`. All ten frozen genes have unique identifiers in the deposited annotation crosswalk. ZFTA is predeclared as the old symbol C11orf95, supported independently by the [HGNC 2021 rename notice](https://blog.genenames.org/newsletters/2021/02/25/Winter_newsletter) and [NCBI Gene 65998](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/gene/65998). No candidate replacement is needed. `matrix-headers-only.json` records headers and URLs. The full source expression/differential files have not been saved; derived annotation-only files have no numerical outcome columns.

The primary article for GSE126458 is [Bertero et al., 2019](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC6719452/), DOI 10.1083/jcb.201902117. The GEO series and sample records are the direct sources for differentiations and exact column names. Samples GSM3602079–81 map to R225R_15_1_0 through R225R_15_3_0; GSM3602082–84 map to R225R_38_1_0 through R225R_38_3_0; GSM3602085–87 map to R225X_1_0 through R225X_3_0.

Lower-priority or excluded intake options:

- [GSE120838](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE120838) is a superseries; RNA subseries [GSE120836](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE120836) has five LMNA-DCM hearts and five control hearts, with age/sex metadata. [Processed XLSX](https://ftp.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/series/GSE120nnn/GSE120836/suppl/GSE120836_RNASeq-DCM_vs_Control.xlsx.gz) availability is recorded, but workbook schema and per-donor abundance are not inspected. Mean ages differ (DCM 50.8 versus control 35.6); both groups have one female. This is a disease/tissue comparison with donor, disease severity, medication and cellular-composition confounding; use as secondary external consistency only after frozen intake.
- [GSE164065](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE164065) is acute mouse day8→10 CM Lmna knockdown, triplicates per group, but the KD/PLKO titles contradict GEO genotype fields. GSM4996015 title KD says genotype WT, and GSM4996018 title PLKO says genotype Lmna KD. The processed XLSX advertises differential-expression data, not proven raw sample counts. Exclude from formal testing until this contradiction is resolved independently of expression outcomes.
- [GSE118882](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE118882) has two isogenic WT/mutant pairs with two libraries per line, but deposits bigWigs rather than a ready per-sample gene matrix. Raw reprocessing would require a separate compute/time decision.
- GSE289418 uses H222P from the same group and cell-system context as already-opened GSE304575. Independence cannot be assumed from a new accession. No outcomes were opened; do not call it a fresh external replication without sample provenance comparison.

Next intake steps, before any values are opened: freeze fixed genes, mapping, dataset order, contrasts, effect thresholds and missingness rules; preserve all failures; set explicit small-n/model limits; then hash full downloaded artifacts and evaluate by the registered analysis. A positive line-level association is still separate from novelty, causal mechanism and wet-lab validation.
