## Biological sources

The cardiac molecular measurements come from Katherine A. Bossone, Xiaobin Zheng,
Lidya Kristiani, Reni Marsela, Youngjo Kim, and Yixian Zheng,
*Lamins gate nuclear and chromatin structures for cardiomyocyte maturation genes*,
2026 preprint, [DOI 10.64898/2026.05.20.726565](https://doi.org/10.64898/2026.05.20.726565),
and [GEO GSE330298](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE330298).
The retained numeric fixture uses [GSE330013 bulk RNA](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE330013)
and [GSE330103 Hi-C](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE330103).

The ESC comparison-context metadata comes from Xiaobin Zheng, Jiabiao Hu, Youngjo
Kim, and Yixian Zheng, *Lamins organize the three dimensional genome from nuclear
periphery in ES cells*, [GEO GSE89520](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE89520).
Those data are prior work and metadata intake, not an independent confirmatory
cohort or a new result of this repository.

The [author-code inventory](../campaigns/lamina-context-pilot/author-code-audit.json)
records the inspected immutable public revision. No license file was found in
that tree. Scripts were read to identify provenance and analysis behavior, but
were not vendored or executed. Article full text is also excluded.

NCBI GEO and the data depositors are acknowledged for public molecular data access.
The [NCBI policy](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/home/about/policies/) places no NCBI
restriction on molecular-data reuse/distribution while retaining possible
third-party rights. Exact source URLs, hashes, extraction rules, and attribution
travel with the retained data. The repository license covers project software,
not an implied relicensing of biological source material.

## Roles still needed for a prospective study

- A participating biology lead to choose an identifiable estimand, assay endpoint,
  and meaningful effect/precision threshold.
- An independent data/evaluation owner to qualify biological units, reserve an
  eligible cohort, enforce evaluator access, and review candidate alternatives.
- A dated prior-art reviewer to assess specific candidate novelty.
- An actual experimental partner, with separately agreed resources and a registered
  discriminatory endpoint, before any mechanistic validation claim.

These are open roles, not commitments attributed to named people. The current
release is complete as software/calibration only; it makes no prospective or
experimental claim on their behalf.
