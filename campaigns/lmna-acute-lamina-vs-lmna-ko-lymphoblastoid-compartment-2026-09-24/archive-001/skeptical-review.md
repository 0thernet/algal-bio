# Skeptical self-review (written before outcome access)

1. **Lineage mismatch is the largest interpretive risk.** GM12878 is a B-lymphoblastoid line; the predictor is cardiomyocyte lamina sensitivity. A null here does not refute the cardiomyocyte finding. The registration pre-commits that reading; it will not be softened after the outcome.
2. **Global contact-scaling change in the KO** (preprint: loop loss, short-range gain). A leading eigenvector can absorb a scaling change. Mitigations frozen in advance: observed/expected normalisation per diagonal, per-chromosome variance-standardised arm in the pass rule, genome SD-ratio flag [0.7, 1.4], per-sample orientation diagnostics. Residual risk: a KO-specific change in the O/E structure that correlates with gene density would inflate or deflate the association uniformly; the rank-partial adjustment for baseline PC1 addresses the part that tracks baseline compartment identity.
3. **Depth imbalance.** KO rep2 has about half the pairs. Low depth adds noise to its PC1 and biases the pooled KO mean toward rep1. The four-pair sign rule exposes this rather than hiding it; if rep2 alone breaks the pair rule the result is "not passed" and that is reported.
4. **Pipeline novelty.** The PC1 pipeline is new code. It is validated before freeze against the deposited HOMER PC1 of two GSE126459 samples with pre-stated thresholds (V1 0.80, V2 0.50); a failure is repaired and recorded. HOMER's method (distance-normalised correlation PCA) differs from ICE/O/E/Pearson; agreement at 500 kb is expected to be high but not perfect, and 0.80 leaves room for method variance while excluding a broken eigenvector.
5. **Liftover.** Midpoint liftover of 10 kb intervals loses ~0.4% of intervals; tiles with <50% coverage are NaN. The predictor distribution on hg19 matches hg38 (SD 0.1947 vs 0.1943; 5,225 vs 5,236 finite tiles). Lost regions cluster at assembly-updated loci, not systematically by compartment.
6. **Floor choice.** −0.05 is set from the null SD (0.019) and the 1st percentile (−0.042), not from the previous observed values; tier B (−0.10) matches the previous registration's floor and is the effect-size claim. Both are frozen before download.
7. **Multiplicity.** One primary contrast (KO−WT), three arms, four pairs, conjunctive. Secondaries are reported only. No arm can be dropped after the outcome.
8. **Untouched status.** No GSE314556 file has been fetched; the only information used is GEO metadata and the preprint text (which reports counts of switching compartments, not tile-level values).
9. **What would make me distrust a pass.** Orientation ρ with TSS density < 0.5 in either WT replicate; SD-ratio flag; the pass driven by one KO replicate; adjusted/unadjusted ratio near the 0.5 floor. All are reported in summary.json regardless.
10. **After round-1 review (pre-freeze additions).** The SD-ratio flag was dead because the eigenvector is unit-scaled per chromosome; replaced by the PC1 eigenvalue share, which is scale-invariant. A per-chromosome cross-sample concordance rule (≥ 0.5 against the WT mean) now catches an eigenvector that captures arms or centromere structure in one sample; more than three excluded autosomes is a PIPELINE_FAILURE, not a result. Verdict labels are computed, not assigned. Byte counts are enforced at run time. The first validation attempt failed on input layout and is retained.
11. **What I still cannot rule out.** That B-cell compartment biology simply does not couple to cardiomyocyte lamina sensitivity; a "not passed" here will be reported as ambiguous, exactly as pre-committed, and will not be re-run with a different cohort under this registration.
